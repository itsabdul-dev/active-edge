# ActiveEdge live QA — 24 September 2026

Target: https://active-edge-website.vercel.app/
Method: interactive browser walkthrough, accessibility/DOM inspection, screenshots, browser console review. Tested at 390px, 664px and 1440px viewport widths. No source changes or deployments made.

## Confirmed findings

### Major: payment claims contradict demo checkout
Pages: homepage, product page, cart, FAQ and checkout.
Steps: open FAQ and expand “What payment methods do you accept?”; then add a product and continue through delivery details to payment.
Actual: header, product instalment copy, cart, footer and FAQ advertise Payflex/card/Ozow/SnapScan acceptance. Only the payment step clearly announces that no payment or order will be processed.
Expected: disclose demo status before customers enter delivery information; remove acceptance claims until the integrations are live.

### Minor: homepage category links do not select the category
Pages: / and /shop.
Steps: click the Women’s category tile on the homepage.
Actual: /shop opens with all 11 items, including men's products. Both homepage category tiles point to the same unfiltered /shop URL.
Expected: Women's opens the women's filter; Men's opens the men's filter.

## Passed checks

- Homepage, shop, Performance Tee product, account, delivery checkout, payment demo, FAQ/size guide and fabric pages rendered.
- Shop showed 11 products; image inspection found no broken images among its 13 image elements. Fabric page images also loaded.
- Women's filter, sets filter, ascending price sorting and colour preview changed the displayed catalogue correctly.
- Medium Performance Tee could be selected and added to the bag.
- Increasing quantity to two produced R598 subtotal + R85 delivery = R683; persisted after refresh.
- Required delivery fields prevented empty submission.
- Synthetic delivery details reached demo payment. Completing the demo explicitly stated no payment, order or confirmation email; bag remained intact.
- Account sign-in, signup and reset forms displayed and switched correctly.
- Mobile menu opened and navigated; sampled phone shop layout showed no obvious clipping of controls.
- No console warnings/errors captured during the walkthrough.

## Observations and limits

Some route transitions and the bag refresh showed delayed content/loading before resolving. This was not a measured performance audit, and no cause was established.

Did not submit a new account, test an authenticated customer's live session, send password-reset/newsletter emails, or make a payment. Account forms displaying correctly does not prove email delivery or end-to-end authentication. Payment remains intentionally a demo. This was a sampled exploratory review, not exhaustive security, accessibility, browser compatibility or load testing.

The synthetic bag item was removed after testing. No real order was created.

Rerun: firecrawl-qa workflow; full storefront focus; same URL. Browser interactions performed with the available Codex browser controls.
