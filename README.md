# ActiveEdge

**Live Website:** [https://active-edge-website.vercel.app/](https://active-edge-website.vercel.app/)

Welcome to **ActiveEdge**, the future of fitness apparel. 

Founded in **2026**, ActiveEdge is a premium activewear brand dedicated to pushing the boundaries of performance clothing while respecting our planet. We believe that you shouldn't have to compromise between high-performance gear and environmental responsibility.

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to provide athletes and fitness enthusiasts with top-tier, durable clothing that leaves no footprint behind. 

We are deeply committed to sustainability. Every piece of clothing we produce uses **100% eco-friendly materials**—from recycled fabrics to organic cottons. Furthermore, we operate on a strict **zero-waste** manufacturing process, ensuring that every scrap of fabric is repurposed and nothing goes into a landfill.

## Why ActiveEdge?
- 🌱 **Eco-Friendly Materials**: Crafted from recycled and organic fabrics.
- ♻️ **Zero-Waste Production**: Our manufacturing processes are designed to eliminate waste entirely.
- 🏃 **High Performance**: Built to withstand the toughest workouts, providing maximum comfort, flexibility, and breathability.
- 🌍 **Sustainable Future**: We started in 2026 with a promise to protect the environment while outfitting the world.
